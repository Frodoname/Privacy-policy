## Summary

- The app has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or other contact details.
- The amounts you enter and your VAT rates stay on your device.
- The app shows banner ads from Yandex (Yandex Mobile Ads SDK, including AppMetrica) and uses Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config). These services collect device identifiers, usage and ad interaction data, approximate location derived from the IP address, and crash/performance data, to show and measure ads, analyze usage and fix crashes.
- The advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change this any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
- The "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Apple; we never see your payment details.
- We do not sell personal data. For any privacy request, email freeway995@gmail.com.
